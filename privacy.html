Privacy policy

Applies to Glass Model Uploader, the tool behind The Glass Model YouTube channel. Last updated 14 November 2026.

Who uses it

Glass Model Uploader is used only by the owner of The Glass Model channel, to manage that one channel. It has no other users and no sign-up.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission, granted through Google's sign-in, it uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos to The Glass Model channel, set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and publish times, and read that channel's YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, subscribers).

What it stores, and where

The sign-in token and the analytics numbers are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. It does not collect data about viewers of the channel.

Google and YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google handles data as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings (remove "Glass Model Uploader"). After access is revoked, or on request, the stored token and analytics data are deleted within 7 days.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/seekalgos/theglassmodel/issues.
